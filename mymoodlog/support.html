Support

Questions, problems, ideas: yasamin.klingler@gmail.com.

A few things that come up:

  • Where is my data? On your phone. See the privacy policy.
  • How do I back up? Review › History › ⋯ › Export backup saves a file you can keep in Files or iCloud Drive; Import backup restores it.
  • Steps are missing. MyMoodLog needs permission to read steps from Apple Health: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › MyMoodLog.
  • The watch doesn't sync. Open the phone app once; the watch queues check-ins until the phone is reachable.
  • Reminders don't arrive. In MyMoodLog go to Settings › Reminders. If it says notifications are switched off, turn them on under Settings › Notifications › MyMoodLog › Allow Notifications. Otherwise check Focus modes and Scheduled Summary on the same iOS screen. A check-in reminder only comes when your last check-in is older than the hours set there, and only between the two times; a medication reminder stops for the day once that med is logged.
